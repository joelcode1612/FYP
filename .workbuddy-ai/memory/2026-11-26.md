  (`""`, `application/octet-stream`, …) no longer counts as a mismatch; the
  magic-byte signature remains the control. New `canonical_mime(ext)`.
- `backend/src/sitewise/services/property_verification_service.py` — stores
  `storage.canonical_mime(ext)` instead of the client's declaration.
