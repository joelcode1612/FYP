SECTION 4 — DECLARED OVERLAP RESOLUTIONS
----------------------------------------
M2V2-02 vs M2V2-15
  M2V2-02 asks for the single intended service format at concept level,
  including HYBRID and UNDECIDED. M2V2-15 asks which formats will actually be
  offered, as a multiselect, with NOT_DECIDED exclusive. They are distinct:
  intent versus offered operation. Both retained.

M2V2-04 vs M2V2-17
  M2V2-04 asks which broad customer groups are intended. M2V2-17 asks for the
  entrepreneur's own definition of each selected segment, plus optional
  descriptions. They are distinct: group selection versus segment definition.
  Both retained; M2V2-17 options are derived from M2V2-04.
